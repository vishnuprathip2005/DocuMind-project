# ACME Global Remote Work & Digital Workspace Guidelines

## Core Principles
Acme Global operates on a flexible hybrid and remote-first model. Our priority is high-trust collaboration, clear asynchronous communication, and work-life balance.

## Core Collaboration Hours
To facilitate cross-timezone coordination, all engineering and product teams are expected to maintain availability during core overlap hours between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST**.

## Home Office Stipend
Every new full-time employee receives a one-time **$1,500 Home Office Equipment Grant** to purchase ergonomic desks, chairs, monitors, and peripherals. Additionally, a monthly **$100 Internet & Phone Subsidy** is reimbursed automatically.

## Security & Compliance
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory on all corporate accounts.
- Company hardware must run approved endpoint protection software.
- Storing customer data or source code on personal unencrypted drives is prohibited.
