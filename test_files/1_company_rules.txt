COMPANY RULES AND WORKPLACE GUIDELINES (2026)

1. WORKING HOURS AND BADGE ACCESS
The main office building is accessible via electronic security badges 24/7. Core office hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Front security locks all guest entrances at 6:00 PM daily.

2. DRESS CODE
We observe a smart casual dress policy. Jeans, t-shirts, and sneakers in clean condition are welcome. Flip-flops and beachwear are not permitted in client meeting zones.

3. PET POLICY
Well-behaved dogs are allowed in the office on "Doggy Wednesdays" provided they are fully vaccinated and registered with building reception. Pets must remain on a leash in common cafeterias.

4. SNACKS AND BEVERAGES
All kitchenettes on floors 2 and 4 are stocked every Monday with organic fruit, cold brew coffee on tap, oat milk, and gluten-free snacks.
