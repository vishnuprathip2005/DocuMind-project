# Product Roadmap 2026: Project Nebula

## Overview
Project Nebula is our next-generation cloud analytics platform built for high-throughput teams.

## Milestones & Release Schedule

### Q1 2026: Mobile App Launch
- **Target Date**: March 15, 2026
- **Platforms**: iOS and Android native apps
- **Key Features**: Push notifications, biometric login, and real-time dashboard widgets.

### Q2 2026: Dark Mode & Collaboration
- **Target Date**: June 30, 2026
- **Key Features**: OLED dark theme, live multi-user document comments, and workspace team channels.

### Q3 2026: Semantic AI Search
- **Target Date**: September 20, 2026
- **Key Features**: Natural language data queries, automated executive summary exports, and PDF parsing.
